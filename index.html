From idea
to launch.

A clear path, shaped around your project.

Understand & agree

We start with your goals, users and existing ways of working. Together, we define the requirements, scope, deliverables, quotation and proposed milestones before development begins.

Design & build

We map the solution and review designs or prototypes with you. Development moves forward in manageable stages, with demonstrations and feedback at agreed checkpoints. Changes to scope are discussed before additional work proceeds.

Test & launch

We test the agreed functionality and support your review before release. Launch planning includes deployment, handover and the documentation or guidance agreed for your project.

Support & improve

Before handover, we agree how ongoing support will work. Future fixes, maintenance and enhancements are reviewed against your needs and the support arrangement.

launch.

Support starts with a clear description of what you need. Email info@machdubit.co.zw with the affected product, what happened and its impact on your work.

Issues & troubleshooting

Report an error, an interruption or unexpected behaviour. We assess its business impact, investigate and agree the next steps, with updates as work progresses. Include steps to reproduce the issue and screenshots where useful; leave out passwords and sensitive customer information.

Maintenance & updates

Maintenance can cover software updates, compatibility checks and routine upkeep. Hosting, backups and monitoring can be discussed where relevant. The services included are set out in your project or support agreement.

Changes & improvements

New features and adjustments are reviewed separately from incident reports. We clarify the requirement and agree scope, cost and scheduling before implementation.

Support coverage, availability and response targets are agreed for each engagement.
